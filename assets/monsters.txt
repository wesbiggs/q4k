# Quest for the King -- the multicolour arena's sprites (src/fight/).
#
# Everything that stands or flies on a battlefield is a multicolour sprite,
# drawn like the prototype's hero in assets/overworld.txt: 8 double-width
# pixels by 16 rows, a square of the field.
#
#   .  nothing      k  black ($D025)      s  light red ($D026: skin, eyes)
#   t  its own colour: a member's tunic, a monster's colour in the bestiary
#
#   sprite <name> [2]      then 16 rows of 8; with 2, 32 rows of 16, for a
#                          body two squares by two (four sprites)
#
# A second block of the same name is its second frame, which it idles
# between; drawn once, tools/mkfields.py makes the second by sinking it a
# row onto its feet. The party's three and the shots are part of the fight
# (src/fight/fight_data.s); each monster is a file on the disk (s.<name>),
# loaded for the fight it is in.
