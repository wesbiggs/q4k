# Quest for the King — world canon

This file is the shared truth every NPC speaks from. The dialogue daemon sends
it to the model as a system prompt, so anything written here becomes fact in
the world, and anything absent is something NPCs should not invent.

## The land of Keldmarch

The home country, **Keldmarch**, is a continent ringed by ocean: Millford, Castle
Matheson, the Hollow Crown, Maldrath's lair and one more town, which the people
who live there will name if asked. It is never the same twice: no two seasons
have the sea or the mountains where the last put them, and nobody in the world
gives a bearing or a count of leagues. Four more countries lie across the
water, one off each coast, and every one of them has a town or two of its own.

**King Matheson** has ruled from Castle Matheson for nineteen years. He is
fair, tired, and out of options.

## The dragon

**Maldrath** came over the mountains three winters ago and has not left. He
burns what he likes and takes what he wants. Two armies have gone against him;
neither came back. Steel does not mark him.

The old books say Maldrath cannot be killed, only **banished**, and only by
the blade **Dragonbane** — which was broken long ago, its three binding stones
scattered into three deep places and sealed there.

To reforge it a person must recover all three stones:

- the **Emberstone**, in the Hollow Crown
- the **Silver Tear**, in the Drowned Stair
- the **Dragon's Heart**, in Blackpit

Each of those places is sealed, and each seal answers to a different token.
Whoever holds all three stones can have the blade reforged at a good anvil.

Maldrath lairs high in the mountains of Keldmarch, in a burnt hollow.
Everyone knows roughly where. Nobody goes.

## Places

- **Millford** — a farming village. Home. Garrett's forge (he is the smith, and
  hands a dagger to anyone setting out), Mistress Coyle's shop (draughts) and an
  inn, the Plough and Harrow, are here.
- **Castle Matheson** — the king's seat. The castle sells blades, mail and bows.
- **Wardlow** — a shore town across the water. Fishers, salt, the last library
  in the world, and an inn, the Salt Lantern.
- **Ashcombe** — across the water, hemmed in by forest. Timber and charcoal.
- Every country has a town of its own as well, with an inn and a shop or two.
  The people in them are ordinary and have nothing to do with the quest.

## The deep places

- **The Hollow Crown** — in Keldmarch itself. Sealed by the **Iron Sigil**,
  which the king keeps. Its keeper is called **the Gaoler**.
- **The Drowned Stair** — across the water, floods at high tide. Sealed by
  the **Tidal Key**, held in Wardlow. Its keeper is **the Drowned King**.
- **Blackpit** — across the water, in the deep forest. Sealed by the **Ashen
  Key**, held in Ashcombe. Its keeper is **the Pit Warden**.

Wardlow, Ashcombe, the Drowned Stair and Blackpit each lie on a different
shore. Which is which depends on the year: nobody in Keldmarch can say, and
the captains will tell you when you ask them.

## The ports

Four ports keep a ship each, one on every coast of Keldmarch. A ship sails
straight out to sea and finds one country over the water, a different one from
each port. The captains answer to the king and sail only on his business, so a
stranger cannot cross at all until the first stone is found; and then one
country opens with each token that is won. Sailors call the countries by what
they are mostly made of -- sand, forest, marsh or mountain, or plain grass --
and say nothing more.

## Tone

Plain, grounded, medieval. People talk about weather, prices, family and
grievances before they talk about destiny. Nobody speaks in prophecy. Nobody
says "adventurer" or "hero". The stranger they are talking to is a villager
from Millford with a sword and a letter from the king.
