# Quest for the King -- town and castle interiors.
#
# Each is its own size, up to 40 wide and 25 tall (TOWN_MAX_W and _H, which
# tools/mktowns.py checks): its rows say how wide, their count how tall, and
# every row must be as wide as the first. The view is 20 squares by 11 (12
# with the text window hidden) and follows the hero, so a town scrolls; what
# lies off its edges is drawn as its tile set's off-map ground.
#
#   town <id> <DISPLAY NAME>
#   ent <char> npc <npc-id>       talks: their lines are assets/talk.txt's
#   ent <char> shop <weapon|armour|item|bow|magic>
#   ent <char> inn <inn-id> <INN NAME>  ale and a bed; the id keys the rumours
#   map                           then the rows, ended by a blank line
#
# Map characters are the tile glyphs from assets/tiles.txt (x, the exit
# tile, is a way out: a town needs at least one), plus:
#   $  where you arrive (drawn as what is beside it)
#   1-6  entity slots declared above
