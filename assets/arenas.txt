# Quest for the King -- battle fields.
#
# Every fight happens on one of these. On the overworld it is picked by the
# square the hero was standing on; below ground it is the floor room; and
# Maldrath has his lair. Each is one viewport, 21x19, like a town. A tile
# with no field of its own gets the first one.
#
#   arena <tile-name> [size]   the tile this field is for; size 2 checks
#                              the monster's start and path for a 2x2 body
#   map                        then 19 rows of 21 characters
#
# Map characters are the tile glyphs from assets/tiles.txt, plus:
#   @  where the hero starts (drawn as whatever is next to it)
#   m  where the monster starts (its top-left square, if it is big)
#
# The monster walks round anything that blocks: trees, boulders, mountains
# and water. tools/mkarenas.py refuses a field where it could not reach you.
