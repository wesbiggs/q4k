# The king's ship's deck, made more of (a fight at sea): the road and the
# clouds, which no field has, give their codes to a mast (white, which
# blocks) and a hatch (a grating in the boards, walked over); and the deck
# has a hull, its edges drawn toward the sea, cut diagonally at its outside
# corners (and a slant drawn whole: the hull tiles below).

uncode dark

code mast 0x96
code hatch 0x94
uncode signinn
uncode signweapon
uncode signarmour
uncode signbow

code hullne 0x9B
