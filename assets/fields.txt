# Quest for the King -- the multicolour arena's battle fields (src/fight/).
#
# One for each kind of ground, as in assets/arenas.txt, but 20 squares by 9:
# a square is a 2x2 metatile, so that is the screen's width and its top 18
# rows. tools/mkfields.py draws each with its tile set, blended edges and
# all, and writes the picture to a file of its own (f.<name>), which the
# fight loads when it is fought on.
#
#   field <name> <tile set> <ground> [size 2] [shut] [scatter=...]
#       <tile set>   a file in assets/, without its .txt
#       <ground>     the tile under the party, the monster and the boulders
#       size 2       checks the start and the path for a 2x2 body
#       shut         no way out at the sides (Maldrath's lair)
#       scatter=oak,puddle:20,...
#                    besides boulders, what the fight may put down afresh
#                    each time (up to three): drawn `on` something in the
#                    tile set; alone it blocks, with :15 or :20 it is
#                    ground that costs that many tenths a step
#
# Each square costs a step 10 tenths (grass, forest, floors), 15 (sand) or
# 20 (swamp, a puddle): the party's members lose a turn for every 10 owed, and the
# monsters spend their pace on it (src/fight/fight.c).
#   then 9 rows of 20 characters
#
# Map characters are the tile glyphs of assets/tiles.txt, plus:
#   @  where the leader starts      m  where the monster starts
#   "  the deep sea (the overworld set's own code, not in tiles.txt)
#
# A `sea` field is fought by the seafight module (src/fight/fight.c built
# with SEA_FIGHT): the monsters keep to the water, which the party cannot
# enter, so the monster starts in it, and no way round is looked for.
#
# The way out is the middle row, open at both ends; mkfields.py refuses a
# field where it is not, or where the monster could not reach the party.
