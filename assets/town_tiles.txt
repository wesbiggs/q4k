# Top left quarter (open to the west, north, both, diagonal, and west and
# north again for the second variant), then bottom left (west, south, ...).
# Above: the roof's back edge. Below: the house's front, the eaves, plaster
# and a beam, and a sill. Beside: the gable's edge. A roof's corner that
# sticks out is rounded off into the grass the houses stand in; the front's
# stay square, since a front may face cobbles or grass, and so do corners
# that go in, which are as often a room's, where the floor is not the grass.
