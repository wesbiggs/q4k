# What the people of Keldmarch say in the offline build (make offline), where
# nobody is on the modem to make it up. tools/mktalk.py packs each section
# into a file of its own on the disk, named by the NPC's index alone (NPC_* in
# include/q4k/town_data.h), and the game loads one when you talk to them.
# The inn rumours are not here: they come from assets/hints.json, the same
# lines the host draws from.
#
#   [npc N]  Name, for the reader only
#   greet STAGES [give] | what they say when you walk up
#   topic STAGES [give] | Menu label | what they say when you ask
#
# STAGES is the quest stage (Q_* in include/q4k/game.h, 0-10): one, a range
# like 2-8, or * for all of them. A greeting is picked at random from those
# that fit the stage, and every stage needs at least one. At most nine topics
# may fit any one stage; Leave is always 0. `give` hands over whatever the
# conversation has earned (quest_after_talk() in src/q4k.c) when the line is
# said, and nothing else does. A line that starts with white space carries on
# the one before it. Plain ASCII only, and keep a line under about 200
# characters: the transcript holds twelve rows. Give no directions -- the
# land is made fresh every game.

[npc 0]  Garrett the Smith, Millford
greet 0     | So the king's letter found you. Nobody leaves Millford without
              steel, so before you go anywhere, ask me for some.
greet 1     | Still here? The castle will not come to you, and it is a long
              walk. Mind that dagger's edge; it is the only one you have.
greet 2     | The king gave you his Sigil, then. Good. A tool is only as good as
              the hand that carries it.
greet 3-6   | You have the look of someone who has been down a hole. Bring me the
              stones when you have all three, not before.
greet 7     | Is that... all three? Put them on the anvil. Carefully.
greet 8     | Dragonbane is the best work this anvil ever did, and I did not do
              most of it. Go and use it.
greet 9-10  | Maldrath banished, with a blade off my anvil. They will drink to
              that for a hundred years. I will drink to it tonight.
topic 0 give | A dagger   | Take it. Nobody walks out of Millford with bare
              hands. Keep it oiled and it will see you home.
topic 7 give | Reforge it | Hold the bellows. Steady. There: Emberstone,
              Silver Tear, Dragon's Heart, and the old steel between them. It
              is done. That is Dragonbane.
topic 0-1   | The letter  | Sealed with the king's own seal, asking for
              someone from Millford. A king does not write to farmers for
              nothing. Go and see what he wants.
topic 2-6   | Dragonbane  | The blade that can banish Maldrath, broken long
              ago. Three binding stones held it together. Bring me all three
              and this anvil will do the rest.
topic *     | Weapons     | Blades, mail and bows you buy at the castle, or in
              a town over the water. A better blade is cheaper than a burial,
              but only just.
topic 0-8   | Maldrath    | Steel does not mark him. Two armies learned that.
              Only Dragonbane can send him off.
topic 9-10  | Maldrath    | Gone, and not coming back while the blade holds.
              My anvil had a hand in that. I will not let anyone forget it.
topic *     | Millford    | Farms, a forge, Coyle's shop and the Plough and
              Harrow. Old Tam will tell you the rest, and then tell you again.

[npc 1]  Mistress Coyle, Millford
greet 0-1   | Draughts, rope, lamp oil. You will want draughts. Everyone takes
              too few.
greet 2-8   | Back again, and still breathing. That puts you ahead of most I
              have sold to.
greet 9-10  | The one who sent the dragon packing, at my counter. I will put it
              in the window.
topic *     | Draughts    | A draught mends what a beast has torn. Drink one
              before a fight goes bad, not after. They cost what they cost
              because the road is long.
topic 0-8   | The road    | Keep to open grass. Forest and swamp are where the
              beasts are thickest. Whoever falls out there gets carried home,
              and wakes with half their purse gone.
topic 0-2   | The others  | Fourth one this year headed for the castle. I
              remember the other three. Two came back.
topic *     | Gossip      | Tam has told the same war story since before I was
              born. Garrett gives daggers away and wonders why he is poor. The
              inn waters its ale. That is Millford.
topic 0-8   | Maldrath    | He took the Harlow flock in one night, two summers
              back, and burned the barn after, as if to make a point.
topic 9-10  | Maldrath    | Banished, they say. I will believe it when the
              price of wool comes down.

[npc 2]  Old Tam, Millford
greet 0     | A letter with the king's own seal, in Millford. That has not
              happened in my lifetime, and my lifetime has been long.
greet 1-2   | Off to see the king, eh? I saw a king once. Not this one; his
              father. Smaller than you would think.
greet 3-8   | You came back. Good. Sit down, I have a story about coming back.
greet 9-10  | I marched against him and lost. You went against him and won. Let
              an old man shake your hand.
topic 0-8   | The army    | I marched south once, with the second army. I
              carried messages. I am the part of it that came back, and I came
              back because I ran.
topic 9-10  | The army    | Five hundred went with me and none came home but
              me. You have paid that debt. I will tell them so, when I see
              them.
topic 0-8   | Advice      | In the open, a gap in the line is a door: walk
              out of a bad fight. In a deep place the keeper bars the door, so
              go down ready.
topic 0-8   | Maldrath    | Steel does not bite him. I saw a good sword go
              across his neck and come away notched. The old books say he can
              only be banished.
topic 0-2   | The king    | Matheson is a fair man and a tired one. Nineteen
              years on the throne, and three of them with a dragon on the
              roof.
topic *     | Old days    | When I was a boy Millford had two forges and no
              dragon. Now it has one forge and a dragon. That is progress.

[npc 3]  Princess Elewyn, Castle Matheson
greet 0-1   | A visitor! Father said someone was coming from Millford. You do
              not look like a knight. The good ones rarely do, in the stories.
greet 2-8   | You have the Sigil! Then you are truly on the quest. Tell me
              everything when you return. Every single thing.
greet 9-10  | You did it! The birds are singing in the forest again; I heard
              them this morning. I will have a song made about you.
topic 0-8   | Knights     | In the old stories a true knight is not the one
              with the best horse. It is the one who goes when nobody else
              will.
topic 0-8   | Your father | He does not sleep. He sends riders, reads old
              books, stares at the mountains. I think he blames himself for
              the armies.
topic *     | My tower    | Father keeps my door locked, for my safety, he
              says. But this castle is old, and old castles have more ways in
              than doors. Not that I would know.
topic 0-8   | Maldrath    | Since he came the birds have gone quiet in the
              forests of Keldmarch. I miss them more than I can say.
topic 9-10  | Maldrath    | Gone! And the birds came back the very next week.
              I knew they would.

[npc 4]  King Matheson, Castle Matheson
greet 0-1   | So you are the one from Millford. I sent two armies against
              Maldrath. I will not send a third. So I am sending you.
greet 2     | You have the Sigil. The Hollow Crown is in our own country. Bring
              me back a stone, not a tale.
greet 3-7   | One stone more than any army brought home. Keep going. The
              captains sail for you now.
greet 8     | Dragonbane. I did not think to see it whole in my lifetime. Go,
              and come back.
greet 9 give | Stand. Maldrath is banished and Keldmarch is free, and the crown
              keeps its promises.
greet 10 give | Our champion returns. The castle is yours to walk whenever you
              wish.
topic 0-1 give | The quest | Maldrath can only be banished, with the blade
              Dragonbane, and its stones are sealed in three deep places.
              The first is the Hollow Crown. Take the Iron Sigil; it opens
              the Crown, and nothing else does.
topic 2-8   | The stones  | The Emberstone in the Hollow Crown, the Silver
              Tear in the Drowned Stair, the Dragon's Heart in Blackpit. Each
              place is sealed, and each seal answers to its own token.
topic 2-8   | The ships   | A ship waits at a port on every coast. The
              captains sail on my business only, and each stone you bring up
              opens one more land to you.
topic 2-8   | The keepers | The Gaoler holds the Crown, the Drowned King the
              Stair, the Pit Warden Blackpit. None of them will let you run.
topic 2-7   | The blade   | Three stones, then Garrett's anvil, then the
              blade. That is the whole of it.
topic 0-8   | Maldrath    | He came over the mountains three winters ago. His
              lair is high in a burnt hollow. Everyone knows roughly where.
              Nobody goes.
topic 9-10  | The kingdom | The fields first, then the walls, then the songs.
              My daughter has started on the songs already. Keldmarch will
              mend.

[npc 5]  Steward Merrit, Castle Matheson
greet 0-1   | His majesty will see you. I advise you to be brief. You are the
              one from Millford. Yes. Well.
greet 2-8   | Back again. His majesty is in the hall, as ever. Do wipe your
              boots.
greet 9-10  | I owe you an apology. I did not believe in the plan. I am pleased
              to have been wrong, which is not a thing I say often.
topic 0-8   | The plan    | Two armies, and now one of you. I did not choose
              the plan. I merely keep the accounts, and the accounts are not
              encouraging.
topic *     | Armoury     | The castle sells blades, mail and bows to those
              who can pay. His majesty does not give them away. I would know.
topic *     | Princess    | Her highness is kept safe in her tower. Her
              highness is also of the opinion that locks are a suggestion. I
              do not discuss it.
topic 0-2   | Captains    | The captains sail on the crown's business only.
              Until there is business they sit in port and complain about the
              food.

[npc 6]  Old Brann, Wardlow
greet 0-8   | Southerner. You will want the library, and the library will want
              quiet.
greet 9-10  | The dragon one. Sit, if you like. The fish do not care who you
              are, and neither, much, do I.
topic 0-8   | The tide    | The Stair floods twice a day. Go at the wrong hour
              and the sea keeps you. We call the hour it runs dry the bone
              tide, for what you see on the steps then.
topic 9-10  | The tide    | The Stair is quiet now. The lights went out the
              week your dragon left. Make of that what you will.
topic 0-8   | The lights  | Lights down there at low water, at the mouth of
              the Stair. I said so once. They laughed. I have not said it
              since.
topic *     | Wardlow     | Salt, fish and books, that is Wardlow. The books
              are Vane's. The fish are mine. The salt is everybody's.
topic *     | Vane        | Clever man. Knows every tide in his tables and not
              one of the words we use for them. Never asked.

[npc 7]  Loremaster Vane, Wardlow
greet 0-2   | A visitor from the south. The library is open, though I warn you
              most of it is about fish.
greet 3     | You have the Emberstone, I am told. Proof you can survive. Now,
              can you listen?
greet 4-8   | Back with the Tidal Key still in your hand? Do mind the hour.
greet 9-10  | Banished, not slain. The distinction matters, and you are the one
              person who will never get it wrong.
topic 3 give | The dry hour | So the fishers call it the bone tide. Forty
              years in this library and nobody told me. Take the Tidal Key,
              and mind the hour.
topic 0-2   | Tidal Key   | I keep it, and I will hand it to someone who has
              brought the Emberstone out of a deep place. Not before. A key is
              a responsibility.
topic 2-8   | The Stair   | It floods twice a day. That is not a legend, it is
              a tide table. Its keeper is called the Drowned King, which is a
              title, not a pedigree.
topic 0-8   | Maldrath    | Banished, not slain. He cannot be killed, only sent
              away, and only with Dragonbane. Everyone gets this wrong.
topic 9-10  | Maldrath    | Banished. I have written it down, with the date
              and the tide. Somebody will get it wrong anyway.
topic *     | The library | The last library in Keldmarch, and I its last
              keeper. Most of it is tide tables. The rest is arguments about
              tide tables.

[npc 8]  Mother Ash, Ashcombe
greet 0-4   | You are not from here. Ashcombe does not get many who are not
              from here, and it likes it that way.
greet 5     | You carry the Silver Tear. I can feel it from here, cold as a
              well. I know what you want.
greet 6     | You have my key. I hope Blackpit gives you back. It does not give
              much back.
greet 7-8   | You went down and came back up. Few do. Do not tell me what you
              saw.
greet 9-10  | The dragon is gone, they say. Blackpit is still there. Some
              things stay shut for a reason.
topic 5 give | Ashen Key  | The Silver Tear buys the Ashen Key. It does not buy
              my blessing. Take it. Blackpit should stay shut, and you will
              open it anyway.
topic 0-8   | Blackpit    | I do not burn within a mile of it. Its keeper is
              the Pit Warden, and the forest round it is thick with things
              that bite. Carry draughts.
topic 0-8   | Charcoal    | Two of my stacks went out overnight. Soaked
              through, in a dry week. Something is wet that should not be.
topic 9-10  | Charcoal    | My stacks burn true again. Whatever was soaking
              them has stopped. I do not ask why.
topic *     | Ashcombe    | Timber and charcoal, and forest on every side. We
              keep to ourselves. The forest keeps to itself. Mostly.
